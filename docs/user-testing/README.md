# Turbolong User-Testing Programme — Test Plan

**Milestone 4 — Testing.** A structured, moderated testing programme run by the
team. Sessions are weighted toward **LatAm** and **DeFi-native** users and cover
the full **deposit → monitor → exit** journey. Every task gets a recorded
success score. Findings are triaged by severity and tracked as labelled GitHub
issues. The results go into a findings report.

This complements [`docs/testing-programme.md`](../testing-programme.md), which
covers automated QA (contracts, parity, E2E). That programme checks the maths
is right. This one checks whether people can use the product safely and
understand the risk they take on.

| Milestone measure | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Structured test plan | This document, plus the task cards in [`facilitator-guide.md`](facilitator-guide.md) and recruitment in [`recruitment.md`](recruitment.md) |
| Completed sessions with recorded task-success scores | One pseudonymised record per session in [`sessions/`](sessions/), validated against [`protocol.json`](protocol.json) |
| Findings report | [`findings-report.md`](findings-report.md). Its results section is generated by `scripts/user_testing_report.ts` |
| Findings triaged by severity, tracked as labelled issues | GitHub issues labelled [`user-testing`](https://github.com/Dgetsylver/TurboLong/issues?q=label%3Auser-testing) + `severity:*` + `flow:*` (§7) |

## 1. What we want to learn

| # | Research question | Answered by |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | Can people get from a fresh wallet to an open leveraged position and a vault deposit **without help**? | O1, D2, D3 success |
| RQ2 | Do they understand the risk **before** they sign: Account Health, liquidation, negative APY? | C1, C2, read at the review modal |
| RQ3 | Once deposited, can they tell how their money is doing and whether it's safe, and set up a warning they'd rely on? | M1–M3, C3, C4 |
| RQ4 | Can they get money out, partly and fully, and do they understand what each exit does? | X1–X3, C5, C6 |
| RQ5 | Do LatAm users in ES / PT-BR, often on mobile, have the same success as English desktop users? Do DeFi-native users hit different problems than newer users? | Cohort splits in the report |
| RQ6 | Which of the 108 heuristic findings in #282, and the known issues in §9, actually hurt real users? | Findings report §5 |

## 2. Scope and environment

**In scope:** the main app (`frontend/`), the trade and vault journeys, the
Dashboard, the alerts subscription and email, and the Status page.

**Environment: testnet, XLM only.** Every scored task runs on Stellar testnet with
a fresh wallet funded by Friendbot. Here's why:

- **Vaults exist only on testnet.** Mainnet vault IDs are empty
  (`frontend/src/defindex.ts`, `MAINNET_VAULTS`), so the vault half of the
  journey can't be tested on mainnet yet.
- **XLM needs no trustline.** It's in both testnet pools and has a testnet vault
  (*Leveraged XLM (Testnet)*). So a Friendbot balance of 10,000 XLM covers
  every core task and no one has to hand participants tokens. The BLND
  trustline the app adds itself is part of what's under test.
- **No real money is at risk**, so we can recruit people who wouldn't put funds
  into a new protocol on day one. Participants also never hand anyone a secret key.

**One exception: M3 (alerts) runs on mainnet.** The alerts modal refuses testnet.
It needs only an email address, no wallet. The facilitator asks the participant
to switch the network pill to Mainnet as setup (not scored), then back afterwards.

**Out of scope for this round:**

- **Selling receipt tokens on Aquarius.** Only the testnet *USDC* vault is listed,
  which would mean giving participants USDC and trustlines. Re-test when mainnet
  listings land.
- **Swap.** Execution is mainnet-only.
- **Push alerts.** No UI registers them.
- **Ledger.** Revisit at mainnet.

## 3. Participants

### Cohort definitions (from the screener in `recruitment.md`)

- **LatAm:** lives in Latin America (Mexico, Central or South America, or the
  Spanish/Portuguese-speaking Caribbean).
- **DeFi-native:** in the last 12 months, has used at least one on-chain lending,
  borrowing or leverage product (Blend, Aave, Morpho, Kamino, a perp DEX…) with
  their own money.
- A participant can be in both cohorts or neither. The `cohorts` field of the
  session record holds the cohorts they're in.

### Sample: 12 completed sessions

| | DeFi-native | Not DeFi-native | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LatAm** | 5 | 3 | **8** (≥ 60% gate) |
| **Not LatAm** | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| **Total** | **8** (≥ 60% gate) | 4 | **12** |

**Languages:**

- **ES:** at least 4 sessions (Mexico, Argentina and Colombia preferred, so we
  hear more than one variety of Spanish).
- **PT-BR:** at least 3 sessions.
- **EN:** the rest.

**Devices:** at least 3 sessions on a phone (LatAm usage is mobile-first),
preferably all LatAm.

**Why 12:** about five participants per segment surfaces most usability problems
that affect a large share of users. Twelve gives at least five in each of the two
weighted cohorts while staying runnable in two weeks. Task-success intervals at
n = 12 are wide (about ±25 points). The report treats differences between cohorts
as directional, not proven.

**Recruitment and screening:**

- Recruit about 16 to allow for no-shows. Outreach, screener and consent texts
  are in [`recruitment.md`](recruitment.md).
- **Exclude:** anyone who works on Turbolong or Blend, anyone who has
  contributed to this repo, and anyone who took part in an earlier session.
  Team members run the pilot sessions only.
- **Incentive:** a fixed thank-you (suggested USD 30 equivalent in USDC on
  Stellar mainnet), paid after the session whatever happened in it.

## 4. Session format

- **Moderated, remote, think-aloud, 60 minutes** (book 75). Video call with screen
  share. Run in the participant's language by a facilitator fluent in it.
- **Two team members per session:**
  - the **facilitator** runs the script and stays neutral;
  - the **note-taker** keeps the camera off, times tasks, scores live and
    timestamps quotes.
- **Recording:** recorded with consent. If the participant declines,
  the note-taker's live notes are the record. Recordings stay in the team's
  private drive (§8), never in this repo.
- **Device:** desktop sessions use Freighter (extension). Phone sessions use the
  mobile path confirmed in the pilot (§9). The note-taker records the wallet
  actually used in the session record.

| Block | Minutes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Intro and consent | 5 | Script, recording consent, "we're testing the app, not you" |
| Warm-up | 3 | Background questions; a 5-second look at the landing screen |
| Onboard | 5 | O1 (core), O2 (stretch) |
| Deposit | 15 | D1 (stretch), D2 + C1/C2, D3 |
| Monitor | 13 | M1, M2 + C3, M3 + C4 (mainnet), M4/M5 (stretch) |
| Exit | 10 | X1 + C5, X2 + C6, X3 |
| Wrap-up | 7 | SUS, confidence, debrief questions, payment details |

Core tasks run in order because each one depends on the state the previous one
leaves. Stretch tasks are dropped first when time runs short. A dropped task is
recorded as `score: null` with a `skipReason`.

## 5. Tasks

The full task cards are in [`facilitator-guide.md`](facilitator-guide.md). Each
card has the prompt in EN/ES/PT-BR, observable success criteria, a time limit,
the scripted hint and what to watch for. Prompts describe goals, not UI labels:
"take 300 XLM out but keep it running", not "use Remove Funds".

| ID | Phase | Scenario (abridged) | Success = observable end state | Limit | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Onboard | Connect your test wallet (wallet already on testnet; app opens on mainnet) | App on Testnet, wallet connected, the Trade deposit card shows their XLM balance | 5 min | core |
| O2 | Onboard | Put the app in your language (only if it isn't already) | All nav labels in their language | 2 min | stretch |
| D1 | Deposit | Find where XLM would earn the most with leverage, and pick one | Names a pool and leverage, justified by **net** APY (not base APY) | 4 min | stretch |
| D2 | Deposit | Open a leveraged XLM position with 1,000 XLM at a risk you're comfortable with | Position confirmed on-chain with a 950–1,050 XLM deposit (leverage and HF chosen are noted, not scored) | 8 min | core |
| D3 | Deposit | Put 500 XLM in the hands-off option | Deposit into *Leveraged XLM (Testnet)* vault confirmed | 4 min | core |
| M1 | Monitor | A week later: what's it worth, what's it earning, how safe is it? | Reads equity, net APY and Account Health of the D2 position correctly | 4 min | core |
| M2 | Monitor | How healthy is the vault, and what happens if it gets risky? | Finds the vault's Strategy Health | 3 min | core |
| M3 | Monitor | Set things up so you'd be warned before liquidation (on mainnet) | A *health factor below* or *liquidation imminent* alert for XLM at the leverage bracket closest to their D2 position, submitted or correctly filled (any pool: mainnet pools differ from testnet) | 5 min | core |
| M4 | Monitor | You're nervous: make the position safer without closing it | Leverage decreased and confirmed | 3 min | stretch |
| M5 | Monitor | Numbers stopped updating: is Turbolong itself having a problem? | Reaches the Status page | 2 min | stretch |
| X1 | Exit | Get 300 XLM back but keep the rest running | ~300 XLM withdrawn, position still open | 4 min | core |
| X2 | Exit | Close the position completely | Position closed, equity back in wallet | 3 min | core |
| X3 | Exit | Take everything out of the hands-off option | Vault withdraw confirmed, ≥ 99% of vault equity returned | 3 min | core |

**Risk-comprehension checks** are asked at the moment they matter: C1/C2 while the
D2 review modal is open, **before** the participant confirms. They are scored
separately from task success, because a participant can complete a task and still
misunderstand what they signed. That is the most dangerous outcome in a leverage
product.

| ID | Question (abridged) | Correct answer must include |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | What does Account Health mean, and what happens if it reaches 1.0? | Measures collateral vs. debt; at or below 1.0 the position can be liquidated and collateral lost |
| C2 | What could make this position lose money? | At least one real driver: borrow cost above supply yield + BLND (negative net APY), Account Health falling toward 1, BLND emissions dropping |
| C3 | Who looks after the vault if it gets risky, and how? | An automatic keeper partially unwinds when strategy health drops below the vault's orange threshold (shown on the vault's Strategy Position card; 1.20 in the testnet XLM config); no action needed from them |
| C4 | What will the alert you just set up be watching? | It watches the chosen pool/asset at the chosen leverage, **not** their wallet's actual position |
| C5 | After taking 300 XLM out, what happened to your leverage and health? | Both stayed the same (proportional unwind) |
| C6 | Before closing: is there anything you'd lose by closing right now? | Unclaimed BLND is forfeited unless claimed first |

## 6. Scoring

### Task score

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **2: success unaided** | Reached the success state within the time limit with no facilitator help. Detours they recovered from on their own are fine. |
| **1: assisted or partial** | Reached success only after the scripted hint, **or** ended in a partly correct state (right product, wrong amount; position opened but they can't say at what risk). Record `assists ≥ 1` or a note saying what was partial. |
| **0: fail** | Gave up, ran past the time limit, needed more than the hint, **or a false success**: believes they're done but the end state is wrong. |
| `null`: not attempted | Skipped for time or because a prerequisite failed. Must have a `skipReason`. |

**Assist ladder:**

1. **Neutral prompt** ("What are you looking for?", "What would you try next?").
   Doesn't count as an assist.
2. **The task card's scripted hint.** Counts as an assist, so the score is at
   most 1.
3. **Telling them or doing it for them.** Scores 0. Only used so the session can
   continue to the next task.

**Recorded per task:**

- `timeSec`: from the end of the prompt to the success state (or to giving up).
- `seq`: the Single Ease Question, 1–7, asked right after the task.
- `assists`: the number of scripted hints given.
- `issues`: the GitHub issues the task's problems were filed under. Filled in at
  triage.

### Other measures

- **Comprehension (C1–C6).** `correct`, `partial` or `incorrect`, against the
  "must include" column above. *Partial* means the direction is right but the
  consequence is missing (for example "lower health is worse" without
  liquidation).
- **SUS.** The 10-item System Usability Scale at the end, in the participant's
  language. Scored 0–100; 68 is roughly average.
- **Confidence.** "How confident would you feel putting your own money into
  Turbolong?", 1–7.
- **End-to-end journey.** The share of sessions where **every core task** scored
  ≥ 1, and where every core task scored 2.

**Headline metrics in the report:**

- Success rate per task: the share of attempts scored 2, with an adjusted-Wald
  95% interval.
- Completion rate: score ≥ 1.
- Median time on unaided successes.
- Mean SEQ.
- All of the above split by phase and cohort.

## 7. Severity and triage

### Severity scale

Severity is set by **impact first**. Frequency can only raise it.

| Label | Severity | Definition | Examples | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `severity:S1-critical` | Critical | Could lead to fund loss or a liquidation the user didn't expect. **Or** the user leaves believing something false about their risk or money. **Or** a core task is blocked with no workaround they could find. | Thinks the alert watches their own position; signs at Account Health 1.02 thinking it's "safe"; can't connect from their mobile wallet at all | Fix before the next release. Raise in the team channel the same day. |
| `severity:S2-high` | High | A core task failed or needed a hint. A serious misunderstanding that doesn't put funds at risk. An error that badly damages trust. | Can't find the vault; raw JSON error at signing; doesn't know the position was opened | Fix this cycle |
| `severity:S3-medium` | Medium | Slowed down, hesitated, or made an error they recovered from. English text in an ES/PT core flow. | Tries to type the leverage; looks for "withdraw" under the wrong tab | Schedule |
| `severity:S4-low` | Low | Cosmetic, wording, translation polish, preference. | Label truncation; "APY" vs "rendimiento" | Backlog |

**Raising severity for frequency:** an S3 seen in half the sessions or more
becomes S2. Severity never goes down for rarity: an S1 seen once is still S1.

### From observation to labelled issue

1. **During the session.** The note-taker logs raw observations with timestamps
   in the private notes.
2. **Within 24 h.** The note-taker files `sessions/P<NN>.json` (scores only, with
   empty `issues`) and runs
   `npx tsx scripts/user_testing_report.ts --no-issues --stdout` to validate it.
3. **Triage, after every 3 sessions** (about twice a week). Facilitator,
   note-taker and a developer:
   - **Dedupe** the observations into distinct problems. One issue per problem,
     not per session.
   - **File** each new problem with the **User-testing finding** issue form
     (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/user-testing-finding.yml`), which auto-applies
     `user-testing`. Add the session ID to an existing issue if the problem is
     already filed.
   - **Label.** Exactly one `severity:*`, at least one `flow:*`
     (`onboard` / `deposit` / `monitor` / `exit`), plus the existing `area:*` and
     `bug`/`enhancement` as usual.
   - **Link back.** Put each issue number into the `issues` list of the task where
     it happened (or the session-level `issues` if it isn't tied to one task).
     Every task scored 0 or 1 must link at least one issue; the report gate
     enforces this.
   - **Regenerate** the report: `npx tsx scripts/user_testing_report.ts`.
4. **If an S1 would confound later sessions** (for example connecting is broken
   for everyone), pause, fix, and record the new `build` SHA in the following
   session records. Note the change under *Deviations* in the report.

Labels are created once with `bash scripts/user_testing_labels.sh`.

The report derives **frequency** ("seen in n/N sessions") and **cohorts affected**
from the session records, so neither has to be typed into the issues.

## 8. Data handling and ethics

- **Consent before recording.** Every participant gets the consent text in their
  language (`recruitment.md`) and can withdraw at any point, including after
  the session. Their record is then deleted.
- **Pseudonymous IDs only** (P01, P02…) in the repo and in issues. No names,
  emails, wallet addresses, recording links or faces. Short verbatim quotes are
  fine.
- **Recordings and the ID ↔ person mapping** stay in the team's private drive
  with access limited to the facilitators. They are deleted 90 days after the
  report is final.
- **Never ask for a secret key or recovery phrase,** and say so up front.
  Participants create a **new** wallet just for the session, in a separate
  browser profile, so their real balances never appear on screen.
- **The M3 email** goes to the alerts service. Participants may use a throwaway
  address, or fill the form and stop before *Subscribe*, which still scores if
  the form is correct. They can unsubscribe with the link in the email.
- **Payment** doesn't depend on performance or opinions.

## 9. Pre-flight

### Known issues going in

Code inspection before sessions turned up issues that will affect specific tasks.
Each has a different consequence:

- **Confounders.** These would make a task measure the bug instead of the user,
  so fix them *before* session 1, or every O1 and D2 result is noise.
- **Watch items.** Real issues the sessions should measure. Leave them in place
  and record how they affect users.

| Issue | Where | Affects | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| After connecting a wallet or switching network, Trade/Vault/Dashboard keep showing the disconnected state until you change tab. The router only remounts on a view change. | `frontend/src/app/router.ts` (`sync`) | O1, D2, D3 | **Confounder: fix first** |
| Testnet banner's "Fund wallet" button has no handler | `frontend/src/app/shell.ts` (`renderBanner`) | O1 | **Confounder: fix** (wire to Friendbot) or hide |
| Onboarding tour styles live in `style.css`, which only the status page imports | `frontend/src/tour.ts`, `frontend/src/status.ts` | warm-up | Fix, or leave the tour out of sessions |
| Toast "View ↗" explorer link is hardcoded to mainnet | `frontend/src/app/chrome.ts` | D2, D3, X* | Fix (otherwise testnet tx links 404 mid-session) |
| Alerts model Account Health per pool/asset/leverage bracket, not the user's position | `alerts/src/index.ts` (`computeHealthFactor`) | M3, C4 | **Watch**: this is what C4 measures |
| Alert verification page always describes the negative-APY alert, even for HF subscriptions | `alerts/src/index.ts` (verify handler) | M3 | Watch / cheap fix |
| Dashboard vault card shows Net APY "+0.0%" because it fetches vault stats without pool reserves | `frontend/src/views/dashboard.screen.ts` | M1, M2 | Watch / cheap fix |
| Trade, Dashboard, modals and toasts are mostly not translated | `frontend/src/views/trade.ts` etc. | all ES/PT sessions | **Watch**: RQ5 measures this |
| Mobile wallets: the WalletConnect deep link is off unless `VITE_WALLET_CONNECT_PROJECT_ID` is set | `frontend/src/app/wallet.ts` | mobile sessions | Decide the mobile path in the pilot (below) |
| No fees or network-cost line in any preview | `frontend/src/views/trade.ts` | D2 | Watch |

The findings report (§5) records whether each heuristic hypothesis, from this
table and from #282, was **confirmed**, **not observed** or **contradicted**.

### Pilot (two sessions, team members, not scored)

1. **EN, desktop, Freighter.** Run the full script, time each block, and confirm
   every core task can be completed on the current testnet. That includes pool
   liquidity for a 1,000 XLM loop and vault capacity for 500 XLM.
2. **ES or PT-BR, phone.** Confirm a mobile signing path on testnet. Albedo is a
   web wallet that works in a phone browser; try it first. Confirm the stepper
   doesn't hide behind the bottom nav. If no mobile path works, file it as an
   S1 for the LatAm cohort and run phone sessions with a phone-width browser
   window plus Freighter on desktop, so the gate reports the shortfall honestly.
3. Fix the script wherever the pilot stumbled, then freeze it. Changes after
   session 1 go under *Deviations*.

## 11. Synthesis

After the last session:

1. Run a final triage and regenerate the report.
2. Hold a 90-minute synthesis workshop. Walk through the S1/S2 findings with
   quotes, and fill in report §5 (hypotheses) and §6 (what worked).
3. Write §1 (summary) and §7 (recommendations) last. Every recommendation links
   to its issue and names the task or check that should improve when it's fixed.

## 12. Done when

Milestone 4 is complete when the generated **Milestone gates** table in
`findings-report.md` is all ✅ **and** the narrative sections are written:

- [ ] ≥ 12 completed sessions; ≥ 60% LatAm; ≥ 60% DeFi-native; ≥ 4 ES; ≥ 3 PT-BR; ≥ 3 mobile.
- [ ] Every completed session has a score (or a reasoned skip) for every core task.
- [ ] Every task scored 0 or 1 links at least one `user-testing` issue.
- [ ] Every `user-testing` issue has one `severity:*` and at least one `flow:*` label.
- [ ] Findings report §1, §2, §4–§8 written; every S1/S2 issue has an owner.
